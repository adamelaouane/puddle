Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Puddle may collect, use, and protect information when you use our marketplace. By using the platform, you acknowledge the practices described on this page.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Puddle may process information such as:

  • Account information, such as your username and email address, if provided.
  • Product information, including descriptions, prices, and uploaded images.
  • Messages you send through conversations on the platform.
  • Technical information required to operate and secure the website.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Display marketplace listings.
  • Enable communication between users.
  • Maintain the security and functionality of the website.

4. Sharing Information

Information you publish, such as your username and product listings, may be visible to other users. Messages may be accessible to the participants in a conversation. Avoid including sensitive personal information in public listings or messages.

5. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate measures to protect information processed by the platform. However, no website or method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

Please keep your account credentials private and contact the website administrator if you have questions about your account or personal information.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our contact page.
